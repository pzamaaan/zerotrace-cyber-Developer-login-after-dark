About Login After Dark

ZeroTrace threat modeling and off-hours authentication anomaly heuristics

Defending the Enterprise During Silent Hours

Adversaries frequently launch automated brute-force, password-spraying, and credential stuffing campaigns during off-hours—between 02:00 and 05:00 local time—when SecOps teams and users are less likely to notice MFA prompts or suspicious lockouts. Login After Dark is an active telemetry sentinel engineered to score, surface, and preemptively mitigate nocturnal authentication anomalies.

1. Off-Hours Velocity Scoring

HEURISTIC 01

Establishes an empirical baseline of normal login windows per identity. Sign-in requests originating outside normal operational timeframes (e.g. 02:00 - 05:00 UTC) receive an amplified risk coefficient (\(+35\%\)) on failure velocity.

Risk_Weight = Base_Risk * (1 + Temporal_Deviation_Ratio)

2. Tor & Proxy Relay Intelligence

HEURISTIC 02

Every ingress authentication handshake is correlated against continuous threat intelligence feeds tracking Tor exit nodes, residential proxies, bulletproof hosters, and known VPN credential stuffing pools.

Flag: ASN_REPUTATION_LOW | TOR_EXIT_NODE

3. Impossible Travel Velocity

HEURISTIC 03

Calculates geographic distance and elapsed time between subsequent successful or failed logins. Multiple attempts from disparate coordinates within impossible transit times trigger an immediate session challenge.

Velocity = Distance_km / Elapsed_Hours > 900 km/h

4. Preemptive Lockout Watch

HEURISTIC 04

Monitors cumulative failed attempts against privileged accounts across distributed IP sources. If a user approaches 70% of the maximum lockout threshold within a 15-minute window, progressive backoff delays and proactive IP tarpitting engage.

Action: PROACTIVE_TARPIT_ENGAGED (Threshold: 10)
